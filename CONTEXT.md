# The Quiet Meridian
A valley tending the last dawn trapped inside a ruined observatory.

## Language
**Wayfarer**: The player's traveler, free to explore the valley.
**Ember**: A dormant fragment of dawn recovered from Mere Shrine.
**Attunement**: Nera's preparation allowing an ember to open the guardian's chamber.
**Meridian heart**: The dawn recovered from the observatory guardian.
**Binding**: Returning dawn to the valley's bell, protecting the settlement.
**Release**: Freeing dawn beyond the valley, opening a new age.
**Relic**: A tradeable recovered object, distinct from protected quest items.
**Recovery**: Returning a fallen wayfarer to Bellwether with possessions intact and a small coin loss.
